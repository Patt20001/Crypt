Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains what personal information NovaWallet collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. By using NovaWallet you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: username, email address, display name, country and a securely hashed password.
  • Verification (KYC) details: first and last name, date of birth, identity documents and a selfie, when you choose to verify your account.
  • Security data: two-factor authentication settings, recovery codes and security-question answers (stored hashed), and recent sign-in information such as IP address, browser and time.
  • Financial activity: balances, deposits, withdrawals, swaps, orders, staking and copy-trading activity, and the wallet addresses you use or save.
  • Communications: support tickets and notifications.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide and secure your account, process transactions and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • To verify your identity and apply withdrawal limits.
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations, including anti-money-laundering requirements.
  • To respond to support requests and send important account and security notifications.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the platform (for example blockchain infrastructure and market-data providers), and with authorities when required by law or to protect users and the platform.

4. Storage on your device

NovaWallet does not use advertising cookies. We store your sign-in session and display preferences (such as theme) in your browser’s local storage so you stay signed in. Signing out clears your session.

5. Security

We protect your data with encrypted connections, hashed credentials, optional two-factor authentication, withdrawal whitelisting and session controls. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and enable two-factor authentication.

6. Retention

We keep your information while your account is active and for as long as needed to meet legal, tax and security obligations. Verification documents are retained only for as long as required for compliance.

7. Your choices and rights

You can update your profile information, manage two-factor authentication and sessions, and remove saved withdrawal addresses from your account. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Submit a request through Contact support; we may need to retain some data where the law requires it.

8. Children

NovaWallet is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from minors.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows when it last changed, and significant changes will be announced in the app.
