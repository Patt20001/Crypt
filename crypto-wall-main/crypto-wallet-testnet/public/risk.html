Risk Disclosure

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Digital assets are highly volatile. You can lose some or all of the funds you use on NovaWallet. Only use money you can afford to lose.

1. Market risk

Prices of digital assets can change rapidly and unpredictably. Trades, swaps and positions may lose value in a short time, and stop-loss or take-profit orders may execute at prices different from the ones you set during fast markets.

2. Pricing and execution

Prices shown on NovaWallet are based on market reference data and may differ from prices on other platforms. Data can be delayed or temporarily unavailable, and quotes may change between the time you see them and the time you confirm.

3. Transfers are irreversible

Blockchain transactions cannot be reversed. Sending funds to the wrong address, on the wrong network, or in an unsupported asset can result in permanent loss. Always verify the asset, network and address before every deposit or withdrawal.

4. Staking

Staking rates are shown per plan and apply to the period selected. Funds in fixed-term plans are committed for that term. Rates may change for new stakes, and returns are not guaranteed to exceed any market losses elsewhere.

5. Copy trading

Copy trading follows a trader’s results and does not guarantee profit. Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results, and you may lose your entire allocation. Performance and platform fees apply to profits. The risk score shown on a trader’s profile is an indicative measure based on volatility, drawdown and win rate; it is not a guarantee or a recommendation.

6. Account and technology risk

You are responsible for protecting your login, two-factor codes and recovery codes. Phishing, malware, network outages, software errors and maintenance windows can affect access to your account or the timing of transactions.

7. Regulatory and tax

Laws governing digital assets differ by country and may change. You are responsible for understanding and complying with the rules and tax obligations that apply to you. Digital-asset balances are not covered by deposit insurance.

8. Not advice

Information on NovaWallet is provided for general purposes and is not financial, investment, legal or tax advice. Consider your own circumstances and seek independent advice where appropriate.
