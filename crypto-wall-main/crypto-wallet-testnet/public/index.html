Frequently asked questions

Which networks and assets are supported?

Deposits and withdrawals are available on TRC20, ERC20 and BEP20. USDT is supported on all three, plus ETH on ERC20 and BNB on BEP20. Additional assets can be traded and swapped inside your account.

Is identity verification required?

No, it is optional, but verified accounts have higher daily withdrawal limits.

What does it cost?

Swaps carry a 0.5% fee. Withdrawals include a network fee that depends on the network you choose, shown before you confirm. Copy trading fees apply to profits only.

How does staking work?

Choose a plan, stake USDT, and rewards accrue daily. Flexible staking can be withdrawn any time; fixed plans (30, 90 and 180 days) pay a higher APY until maturity.
